[product-tour.gif](product-tour.gif) presents four actual screenshots in sequence, with a total duration of 16 seconds and automatic looping. Captions are in Portuguese and English.

1. Desktop diary: `assets/screenshots/daily-dashboard-desktop.png`.
2. Mobile search: `assets/screenshots/food-search-mobile.png`.
3. Favorite meals: `assets/screenshots/favorite-meals-mobile.png`.
4. Individual offline entry: the top portion of `assets/screenshots/offline-food-entry-mobile.png`, labeled as an excerpt in the animation.

The GIF presents existing screenshots rather than recording interaction. It contains no simulated clicks or fabricated state transitions. Screenshots come from different development iterations; see the [source notes](../screenshots/README.en.md).

The animation is 960 × 720 pixels. The offline frame highlights the notice that batch confirmation needs a connection and the separate action for individual offline entry.
