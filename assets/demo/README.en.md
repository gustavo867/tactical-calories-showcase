# Animated product tour

[Back to the showcase](../../README.en.md) · [Português](README.md)

[product-tour.gif](product-tour.gif) presents six actual screenshots in sequence, totaling 24 seconds (4 seconds per frame) with automatic looping. Captions are in Portuguese and English.

1. Desktop diary: `assets/screenshots/daily-dashboard-desktop.png`.
2. USDA catalog with tilapia: top excerpt of `assets/screenshots/catalog-tilapia-mobile.png`.
3. Copy-yesterday shortcut: excerpt of `assets/screenshots/copy-yesterday-desktop.png`.
4. Source and destination review: dialog excerpt from `assets/screenshots/copy-yesterday-review-desktop.png`.
5. Editing owned public and private foods: excerpt of `assets/screenshots/my-foods-desktop.png`.
6. Individual offline entry: top excerpt of `assets/screenshots/offline-food-entry-mobile.png`.

The GIF presents existing screenshots rather than recording interaction. It contains no simulated clicks or fabricated state transitions. Captures come from different development iterations; the catalog, copy, and My foods frames show v0.7.0 features confirmed on main. See the [source notes and full PNGs](../screenshots/README.en.md).

The animation is 960 × 720 pixels. Excerpts are labeled on each frame and resized for presentation without changing interface values or controls. The offline frame retains the notice that batch confirmation needs a connection and the separate action for individual offline entry. The GIF was assembled with Pillow from local PNGs, and all six frames were inspected after encoding.
